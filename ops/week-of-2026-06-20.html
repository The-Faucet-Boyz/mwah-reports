⭐ The headline: Ops now has a real dashboard
The pieces that change how the team sees the business at a glance.
🏠

A live landing dashboard with an at-a-glance Overview

Open Ops and land on a real dashboard that summarizes the business right now, instead of an empty placeholder. The Overview pulls live numbers from synced data.

Dashboard
🔎

Drill into Stores, Products, and Team performance

New analytics views break performance down by store, by product, and by sales rep — so you can see who and what is moving, not just the totals.

Analytics
💧

A "Money" tab that shows where the money goes

The profit picture moved into a dedicated Money tab with a top-to-bottom waterfall, so you can follow the flow from sales down to what's left.

Profit
📊 Charts and tables you can actually read
The data surfaces got a lot more legible and interactive.
📈

Charts now show a scale and exact values on hover

Bar charts gained gridlines and labels so you can read the scale at a glance, and hovering any bar reveals its exact value.

Charts
🔃

Sort, filter, and page through the big tables

The long money and sales tables can now be filtered, sorted by column, and paged — so finding a row in a long list is quick.

Tables
✨

Smoother loading and steadier layouts

Pages now show tidy loading placeholders while data arrives, chart sizing is consistent, and the layout stays centered on wide or zoomed-in screens.

Polish
🧾 Invoices and expenses got smarter
Less manual entry, more tolerance for messy real-world paperwork.
📥

Drop in Excel invoices, not just PDFs and images

Invoice intake now accepts .xls and .xlsx spreadsheets and reads them automatically, alongside the existing PDF and photo support.

Invoices
🗂️

Tag what an invoice was for

Recorded invoices can be categorized — deliveries, marketing, product, and more — and there's now a clean view of everything already recorded.

Invoices
📝

Add notes to expenses, and export the ledger

Logged and recurring expenses can carry notes, the spend ledger has a clearer drilldown, and you can export it to a spreadsheet.

Expenses

Navigation, sign-in, and everyday experience improvements.
🗺️

Navigation reorganized around how you work

The sidebar is now grouped by workflow with collapsible sections, so the area you need is where you'd expect it.

Navigation
🌙

Dark mode

The whole app now has a polished dark mode you can toggle from the top bar — easy on the eyes, day or night.

Experience

Sign in with email, and request access in a click

You can now sign in with an email and password alongside Google, and new teammates can request access right from the sign-in screen.

Access
🔌 Supply chain and live data came online
New visibility into inbound product, plus a steadier connection to the CRM.
🚢

Track inbound shipments

A new Inbound Shipments page shows product on its way in — the first piece of end-to-end visibility from order to shelf.

Shipments
🗺️

A live map of the supply pipeline

An animated flow map traces product from source to store, giving the whole pipeline a single picture.

Pipeline
🔄

The connection that mirrors orders and stores from the CRM is more reliable, and changes made there now flow through cleanly.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Laid the groundwork for automated payment reconciliation and accounts-receivable tracking (the engine that will check payouts line by line).
  • Stood up a reporting warehouse so future analytics can run over the full history of the business.
  • Refreshed the design foundations — a token-based theme that powers the new dark mode and a calm, consistent motion system.
  • Hardened the platform: safer agent access controls, sturdier invoice reading on messy files, and deploy-safety guardrails.
  • Upgraded the backend runtime and tightened how the app prompts you to refresh when a newer version ships.
