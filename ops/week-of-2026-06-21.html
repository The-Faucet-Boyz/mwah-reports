📋

💰 Money & profit got cleaner numbers
More accurate costs, clearer labels, real margin at a glance.
💳

Store credit now shows on the P&L

Credit applied to orders is now surfaced on the profit view and revenue is labeled "net of credits" — so there's no guessing what's included in the number.

P&L
📈

Margin overlaid on Stores, Products, and Team

Gross margin is now shown directly on the Stores, Products, and Team tables — see which parts of the business are pulling their weight without leaving the page.

Margins
🧮

Bulk cost entry unblocks real profit numbers

Enter product costs in bulk to replace placeholder zeros with real figures — the linchpin that turns the Profit view from empty to useful.

COGS
🏷️

Order-level discounts flow through to invoices

Discounts applied to individual orders now carry through to the invoice, so the cost view stays accurate instead of silently over-reporting.
