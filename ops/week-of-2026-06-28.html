⭐ Three things that changed how the team works
The biggest user-facing wins of the week.
📬

Forward an invoice email — it shows up in the review queue automatically

The email intake pipeline is now live. Vendors send invoices to a dedicated address; Chronicle picks them up, extracts the line items, and drops a draft into the review queue with a Slack notification. The team confirms with one click — no more manual entry.

Invoices
📦

Component SKU catalog is open for business

A new catalog tracks the individual components and materials that go into finished products — packaging, inserts, hardware, and so on. As invoice actuals come in, costs roll up to the component level automatically, connecting supply chain to product costing.

Supply Chain
🌎

California orders and vendor inventory are now syncing

A new state integration brings California distributor orders into Chronicle alongside the existing WA data. Vendor-managed inventory (VMI) is mirrored in the same sync, so the team can see what's on consignment and what's been pulled through — in one place.

🧾 Invoice review got a lot more powerful
Seven improvements to how invoices are captured, reviewed, and confirmed this week.
💬

Confirm invoices right from the Slack card

When an invoice arrives, a Slack card lets the team categorize, confirm, or reject it without touching the app. A dropdown right in the card picks the operational category (utilities, supplies, packaging, etc.) before confirming.

Invoices
✏️

Reviewers can add, remove, or edit any line item

The review screen is fully editable now. Add a missing line, remove a duplicate, or correct an amount — all before hitting Confirm. Line amounts and sub-cent unit prices are supported.

Invoices
💳

Payment-processing fees tracked separately

If a distributor charges a card processing fee on top of the order total, it now appears as its own line in Chronicle — so the cost record reflects what was actually paid.

Invoices
🔢

Unit prices shown at full precision

Per-unit prices on invoice line items now display four decimal places, so high-volume items where fractions of a cent matter are legible and accurate in the review screen.

Invoices
🗂️

Source document visible during review

The original invoice PDF or image now appears alongside the extracted data on the draft review screen — so reviewers can compare the AI's read against the source in a single view without switching tabs.

Invoices
🏷️

Smart category pre-fill on every invoice

Chronicle now guesses the right operational category for each invoice based on the vendor and line descriptions. The guess pre-fills the dropdown so confirmation is usually one click, but the reviewer can always override.

📥

Inbox panel shows all email-sourced invoices

A new Inbox panel on the Invoices page lists every invoice that arrived by email — including ones flagged for a second look (unknown sender, missing attachment, etc.) — so nothing slips through.

🐞 Fixes and polish
Five things that were quietly wrong and are now right.
📅

Spend chart month grouping was broken — fixed

An invalid date format was causing some expense and invoice entries to land in a catch-all bucket instead of their correct month. The chart now groups by the right period.

Fix
🚚

Shipping rows no longer appear as line items

When AI extracted invoices with embedded shipping or freight rows in the line-item table, those rows were landing as regular line items. They now correctly roll into the shipping total.

Fix
📋

Email invoices now show in the "To review" queue

Drafts created from incoming emails weren't appearing in the review queue — only uploaded invoices were. Both sources now show correctly.

Fix
⚠️

Invoice confirmation errors are specific now

When a confirm fails validation, the app and Slack both now show the actual reason — instead of a generic "something went wrong" — so the reviewer knows exactly what to fix.

Fix
👥

Owners can manage team access again

A permissions gap was blocking active owners from adding or removing team members. Fixed — user management works as expected for anyone with owner access.

Fix
