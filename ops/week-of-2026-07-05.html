⭐ Three big things landed this week
The features that change how the team works day-to-day.
📋

Purchase orders are now fully manageable end-to-end

POs can be created, edited, confirmed with a supplier, and marked received — all in one place. The full procurement lifecycle is now tracked in Ops, not a spreadsheet.

Procurement
📦

Inventory levels update automatically, every few hours

On-hand and reserved quantities are now pulled in on a regular cadence from your supply partners. The numbers you see in Ops reflect what's actually available — no manual refresh needed.

Inventory
🧮

The platform now suggests how much to reorder

When you're about to place a new PO, the platform computes a suggested quantity based on how fast product has been moving, what's already reserved, and what's already on its way in. Takes the guesswork out of procurement.

Planning
🧾 Invoices and expenses got smarter
Less manual data entry; more complete cost records.
🏢

Vendor profiles are created automatically when you record an invoice

Recording a new invoice now creates or updates the supplier's profile automatically — no separate step required. Vendor records stay current as invoices come in.

Vendors
✈️

Air vs. Sea freight is tracked on every invoice — including existing ones

Each invoice can now be tagged with its freight method (Air or Sea), and that split was applied retroactively to all invoices already on file. Landed-cost tracking now captures how goods actually arrived.

Spend
🔌 Integrations keep more data current automatically
Live connections mean fewer manual lookups.
🚢

Inbound shipment status updates automatically from your freight tracker

When a shipment moves, Ops now reflects the new status without any manual steps. Inbound shipments stay current as packages travel.

Shipments
🔔

The team gets a Slack alert whenever an inbound shipment changes status

Automatic notifications land in Slack each time an inbound shipment's status changes — so the right people know what's arriving without checking a dashboard.

Notifications
🐞 Fixes and polish
Things that now work the way they should.
🖼️

Inbound shipments view cleaned up and polished

A design audit pass landed on the inbound shipments screen — cleaner layout, better spacing, and a more consistent look across tables and status indicators.

UI
🔧

CA inventory and invoice data now comes through complete and in order

A paging issue was causing the first batch of CA orders and invoices to be silently skipped on every sync. That's fixed — all records now flow through correctly.

Fix
⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • A Bill of Materials engine was added — supports multi-level product compositions, which feeds into the reorder and landed-cost calculations.
  • CA inventory connection now live with real data, daily reconciliation, and freshness checks so stale reads surface immediately.
  • CA product catalog seeded so all CA inventory entries and invoices resolve to the correct SKUs.
  • Several data reliability improvements: corrected sync flows that were silently missing records due to off-by-one paging.
