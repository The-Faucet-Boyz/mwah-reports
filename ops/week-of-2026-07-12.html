⭐ The biggest things that landed this week
Three changes the whole team will feel right away.
🎛️

Chronicle's scope selector was completely rebuilt

The business-and-state context switcher at the top of the app is new from the ground up. Labels are now human-readable, the layout works cleanly on both wide and narrow screens, and it's consistent everywhere you look.

Navigation
📋

All invoice lines are now in one unified ledger

Cost lines across invoices are consolidated in a single view with easier filtering. You can also now see and manage which email addresses are trusted to submit invoices — all from the same place.

Invoices
💬

Slack is now two-way — replies come back in

Notifications sent to Slack now track replies and settlement back into the platform automatically. The conversation and the record stay in sync without anyone having to copy things manually.

Integrations
💰 Spend tracking got smarter
Two additions that make expense management more flexible and more visible.
🏷️

Subcategories can be managed directly in the app

Add new expense subcategories inline from the spend screen, and edit or archive them as your categories evolve — no configuration file needed, no engineering required.

Spend
🔍

Untagged spend now has its own view

A new lens surfaces expenses that haven't been categorized yet, so you can see at a glance what still needs attention before closing out a period.

Spend
🧾 Invoice workflows expanded
Several quality-of-life upgrades to how invoices are reviewed, recorded, and tracked.
⚠️

Duplicate invoices are flagged before you save

When you review an invoice, the platform now checks whether a similar one already exists and surfaces an advisory warning — helping catch accidental double-entry before it's in the books.

Invoices
💳

Deposits can be recorded at review time

During invoice review, you can now log a partial deposit payment directly on the record. It's stored as an immutable payment line, keeping the ledger clean and auditable from day one.

Invoices
📩

Trusted invoice senders are automatically recognized

Once an email address is confirmed as a known sender, future invoices from that address are automatically associated with the right source — no manual matching on each new message.

Invoices
🔌 Integrations and sync are more reliable
Fixes and upgrades to how data flows in from outside the platform.
🔄

Product catalog sync is more accurate

The catalog sync was updated to correctly scope products by business and to properly identify which items are available for sale — reducing mismatches between what the platform shows and what's actually in the catalog.

Sync
✅

Handled inbox items can now be resolved

Email inbox attention rows that have been dealt with can now be dismissed and cleared from the active queue, keeping the inbox focused on what still needs action.

Inbox
🧭 The app is easier to move around in
Layout, sidebar, and visual consistency improvements across the board.
📐

The sidebar fits on screen without scrolling

The desktop navigation sidebar was tightened so all menu items are visible at once — no more scrolling down to find the section you need.

Navigation
🎨

Design consistency swept across key screens

Spend, expenses, vendors, and settings screens went through a round of visual polish — headers, spacing, and typography are now consistent throughout the app.

Design
📊

Activity feeds show what's been happening

An inbound activity log now surfaces recent platform events — so you can see a running history of what came in and when, without digging through individual records.

🐞 Fixes that were quietly annoying
Two bugs squashed that were easy to hit in normal use.
🧾

Invoice review form no longer gets stuck

A bug that blocked invoice submission with a "quantity required" error — even when all lines were fully filled out — has been fixed. Review flows as it should again.

Fix
📶

Temporary connection hiccups now recover silently

Brief network errors that were surfacing as visible error messages now retry automatically in the background — fewer interruptions for the same underlying momentary glitch.

Reliability
⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Performance audit across all screens — pages load faster and hold less memory under load.
  • All major platform dependencies updated to current stable versions (validation library, router, linter, TypeScript).
  • Error monitoring tuned to only capture genuinely unexpected problems — fewer false-alarm alerts for the on-call rotation.
