⭐ Three big things landed this week
The most impactful changes the team will feel right away.
📦

End-to-end receiving is live

Shipments now come in with per-line check-in, short-lane tracking, PO linking, and batch IDs — all in one screen. You can see exactly what arrived, what was short, and what needs follow-up without switching tools.

Receiving
🚨

Stockout prevention is live

The platform now forecasts what's running low before you run out. A risk strip, events panel, and margin alerts show which items need attention — with lead-time and safety-stock baked in so the warning comes early enough to act on it.

Planning
💳

Vendor payments have a canonical ledger

One payment record, three ways to view it: a running ledger, a cost breakdown, or a full history. No more hunting across screens to reconcile what was paid and when.

Payments
💰 Spend tracking got sharper
More precise cost categories and cleaner views across the board.
🚚

Freight and delivery are now separate lines

Inbound freight (what it costs to get goods here) and outbound delivery (what it costs to get orders to customers) are tracked as distinct categories. Easier to see where each dollar is going.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Upgraded to React 19 and ESLint 10 — faster builds, cleaner error signals for the dev team.
  • Wired up the merge queue so code changes serialize before landing — fewer surprise conflicts.
  • Added step-tagged telemetry on all financial flows for easier debugging when something looks off.
  • Every cloud function now declares its memory floor — prevents silent out-of-memory failures in production.
  • End-to-end test suite expanded to cover the full callable transport path.
