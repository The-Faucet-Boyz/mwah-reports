⭐ The week's biggest wins
The three changes that move the most for day-to-day work.
🔑

Manage teammate Claude agents from inside the platform

Team members can now connect their Claude agent to the platform with a guided onboarding flow and a secure copy-paste hand-off — no engineering intervention needed. The connected agent list also cleans up stale entries automatically and paginates cleanly as the team grows.

Access
📄

Purchase orders now include full shipping details

Every PO now has a SHIP TO section that can be edited before export. The vendor block is also tightened up — only what vendors actually need. POs are now ready to send directly without manual editing.

Procurement
💵

Record cash transactions directly in the platform

Manual cash account entries are now supported. This unblocks the Cash Flow and Balance Sheet views — which depend on cash entries being captured alongside the rest of the books.
