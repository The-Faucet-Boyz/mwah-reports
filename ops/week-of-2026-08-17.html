⭐ This week's biggest additions
The three changes with the most impact on day-to-day work.
🧾

Purchase orders now carry permanent reference numbers

Every order gets a durable, platform-owned PO number that doesn't change. Vendors receive a fully fillable invoice form matched to the order, cutting the back-and-forth on missing or mismatched line items.

Procurement
📦

Inventory now calculates the transfer shipping plan on screen

The carton count and pallet layout for any transfer are derived and displayed directly in the inventory view. No more manual math before scheduling a move.

Inventory
🔗

Invoice receiving flows straight into the shipment record

When goods arrive and invoices are processed, the receiving step hands off directly to the shipment. The two steps are joined into one continuous path instead of two separate entries.

Receiving
📊 Dashboards give clearer signals
The command center and planning views now surface more useful context at a glance.
🎯

The command center groups work by priority and marks months in progress

Top-of-screen items are now split by tier so the most time-sensitive work stands out. The headline steadies as data loads rather than jumping around, and any month that isn't fully closed is clearly marked as partial.

Dashboard
📈

Planning flags surplus stock, velocity trends, and catalog drift

The planning view now surfaces units that could be transferred out to another market, highlights orders running ahead of typical pace, and alerts when a product in the plan has drifted from the active catalog.

Planning
🧭 Screens are clearer and easier to orient in
A guided tour and consistent screen states make the platform easier to navigate.
🗺️

A guided tour walks through the full supply-chain flow

New team members — or anyone reorienting after an update — can follow a step-by-step tour that covers the supply chain from ordering through receiving. It's hosted and always up to date.

Onboarding
🔲

Loading, empty, filtered, and error states all look different now

Every screen now tells you exactly why a list is empty — whether data is still loading, nothing exists yet, a filter is hiding results, or a read failed. Each state has the right next action, so there's no guessing.

Experience
🐞 Fixes and polish
Smaller corrections that smooth out the rough edges.
🐛

Purchase order flows no longer lose their place mid-session

Navigating between steps in a purchase order previously reset the screen context. That's fixed — picking up where you left off is now reliable.

Fix
⚙️

Markets can now record a confirmed zero tax rate

A zero rate was previously treated the same as an unset one. A market that genuinely has no revenue tax can now record that explicitly, and the platform recognizes it as intentional.

Settings
📄

Vendor PO PDFs show a cleaner goods table with an item count

The goods table in the vendor purchase order PDF is now flattened and includes a total item count, making it easier to review at a glance before sending.

PDF
🏷️

PO component references correctly show which market owns each item

Components on a purchase order are now classified by their owning market. Cross-market references no longer appear mixed in with local ones.

Fix

  • Server library and dependency updates across the platform and backend functions.
