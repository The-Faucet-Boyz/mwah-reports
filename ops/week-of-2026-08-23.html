⭐ Two big wins landed this week
The changes the team will feel most in day-to-day work.
🛒

Pick exactly what goes on a run

A new à-la-carte mode lets you add items to an order without needing a product target — useful for standalone component runs like bags or boxes. Just pick what you need and the system orders that, nothing more.

Ordering
🏭

Procurement controls overhauled

A cluster of procurement fixes shipped together: order quantities now reflect exactly what you enter (no automatic buffers added), WA and CA orders split cleanly in the interface, the proforma invoice form is more accurate, and the inbox is easier to work through.

Procurement
🧾 Vendor documents print right, every time
Multiple fixes to how purchase orders and proforma invoices are generated.
📄

Our data on vendor documents is now real ink

Values on purchase orders and proforma invoices used to be drawn as editable PDF form fields — which means a PDF viewer could reformat them with its own font sizes and clipping. They are now rendered as fixed text: what we print is what the vendor sees, regardless of which PDF viewer they use.

Documents
✂️

Text never gets cut off on a vendor document

Long values on purchase orders now wrap instead of being truncated mid-word. A cut part number or quantity is no longer possible — the text wraps to keep the full value visible.

🧭 The ordering workflow is clearer to use
Quality-of-life improvements to Plan Run controls and product catalog coverage.
💡

Greyed-out actions now explain themselves

When a button in the ordering workflow is unavailable, it now tells you why — no more guessing whether the run is missing something or whether you're in the wrong state.
