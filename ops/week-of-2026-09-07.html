⭐ Three big things landed this week
The changes the whole team will feel right away.
🏭

Production costs now flow into inventory history

When you convert raw materials into finished products, the cost of those inputs is now carried forward through every inventory movement. You can see a complete cost trail from purchase all the way to shelf.

Manufacturing
🤝

Receiving is now connected to your purchase orders

When goods arrive, you can match each item to its original purchase order line. The platform checks quantities, flags discrepancies, and captures the check-in in one place instead of across separate screens.

Receiving
📋

Invoice review now has full version history and smart inbox routing

Every invoice keeps a complete edit history so you can see exactly what changed and when. Incoming invoices are automatically sorted into the right inbox based on their content, cutting down on manual triage.

Invoices
🧾 The invoice experience is more accurate and easier to use
A week of focused improvements to how invoices are read, reviewed, and understood.
🏷️

Each invoice line shows which market it belongs to

State-level cost breakdowns now happen automatically. A company-wide invoice is split by market so each state's spend view only includes what actually belongs to it.

Markets
🔗

Invoice lines are matched to the product they feed

The platform now connects each invoice line to the catalog product it supplies. Subcategory and cost context fill in automatically, with no manual entry needed.

Auto-match
🗂️

The invoice layout is cleaner and easier to scan

Product codes are stacked under item names, market tags sit inline, and the source document stays visible while you review. The subcategory column is wider and everything aligns properly.

Layout
📦

Inventory value is preserved as stock moves through FIFO

Receipt costs now stay attached to each unit as it moves through the warehouse, so cost of goods always reflects actual purchase prices rather than averaged or approximated figures.

Inventory
💳

Credit rows in invoices are recognized correctly

A discount or credit line was previously being dropped from cost breakdowns, causing totals to not reconcile. Credits are now classified and folded into the right place so the numbers add up.

Fix
🔌 Purchase orders and shipments are more tightly connected
Data now flows between receiving and procurement without manual bridging.
📦

Shipment check-in links to purchase order lines

When a shipment arrives, each item can be matched line-by-line to its original purchase order. Quantities and any shortfalls are reconciled in one step.

Sync
🗺️

Invoice market data flows through to the spend ledger

Market tags set on invoice lines now drive the ledger columns correctly, so spend by state reflects each line's actual market rather than the invoice's overall home.

Ledger
🧭 Navigation and key views are faster and more consistent
Layout and filter improvements that affect everyday use.
🔍

Spend filters are unified across the whole area

All filter controls in the Spend section now behave consistently. The vendor sidebar and the ledger stay in sync as you filter, with no duplication or mismatch between panels.

Spend
📐

The app runs at a tighter density with a wider ledger

The app now scales to 80% density, giving data-heavy screens more room. The ledger is also wider, showing more columns without needing to scroll horizontally.

Layout
🏃

Plan-Run has market filters and groups by component type

When building a production run, you can now filter by market and see flavors organized by their component type, making it much easier to find and select what you need.

Plan Run
🐞 Reliability fixes across invoices and expenses
Problems that were causing incorrect data or broken flows are resolved.
✅

Invoice queue approval now works reliably

Duplicate approval reasons and queue state issues that were blocking invoices from being approved are resolved. The approval flow completes cleanly.

Fix
💸

Recurring expenses and mileage history are restored

A set of recurring vendor expenses was being omitted due to a matching gap. The affected entries are back in the right place, and historical mileage data has been repaired alongside them.

Fix
🔧

Purchase matching is more consistent during invoice review

The invoice review flow now uses a single consolidated path for matching purchases to entries, eliminating cases where valid matches were not being recognized.

Fix
