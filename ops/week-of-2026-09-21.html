⭐ The biggest wins this week
The top three things the team will feel right away.
📬

The vendor inbox got a complete overhaul

Held vendor mail is now readable right inside the app. Owners can adjust trust settings for any sender. Conversations collapse into threads so the inbox stays scannable, and held messages can be re-checked at any time. A new spam prefilter also stops irrelevant automated mail from cluttering the queue.

Inbox
👕

The merch catalog is fully built out

You can now define kit recipes and assemble finished items from their component parts. Catalog entries show real tags, out-of-stock status, and let you upload or replace product images. Allocation tracking and a reconciliation view are live alongside a redesigned catalog table and detail page.

Merch
🔍

Drill into every item behind a spend row

The "Where the money goes" view now lets you click any category row to see every individual line item behind it. Each item links directly to its originating invoice so you can trace spending all the way to the source in one click.
