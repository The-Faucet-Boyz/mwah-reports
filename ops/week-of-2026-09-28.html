⭐ Three things worth calling out
The biggest user-facing changes of the week.
🔐

A dedicated sign-in screen for Ops

The Ops platform now has its own sign-in page, styled to match the CRM with the Chronicle indigo look. No more generic login — it feels like the rest of your toolkit from the first click.

Sign-in
📦

Inventory now shows what's committed and what's incoming

Two new columns on the Inventory overview let you see how much stock is reserved for open orders (Committed) and how much is on its way (Incoming). The page also gained direct links to P&L breakdowns and a new shortcut menu you can open with ⌘K.

Inventory
🚚

Shipments page rebuilt from the ground up

The Shipments page has a completely new design. Each row opens a detail popup so you can review a shipment without leaving the list. Shipment names are cleaner and more consistent throughout.

Shipments
💰 Product catalog and cost tracking got smarter
Changes that improve how purchases and products connect to profit.
🗂️

Catalog: tabs, inline editing, and orderable products

The product catalog now has state tabs so you can switch between All, Active, and Archived products without leaving the page. Products can be edited inline, their sort order can be rearranged directly in the list, and reference costs now appear on a single tidy line.

Catalog
🎯

Invoices now match to POs by the market they're for

When an invoice is linked to a purchase order, the match is now made based on which market that line is intended for — not just where the PO happens to be filed. This produces more accurate cost association across locations.

PO Matching
🧾 Invoice intake is more reliable and easier to review
Multiple improvements to how bills arrive, get read, and are presented for review.
🛡️

Duplicate bills are caught before they reach review

Two separate safeguards now prevent the same bill from appearing twice. If a vendor re-sends an identical email, only one draft is created; later copies are filed as duplicates. Bills that are identical matches during ingestion are held for triage rather than added as new entries.

Invoices
🔍

More complete data extracted from incoming bills

The system can now read significantly more content from complex vendor PDFs and emails in one pass. Longer invoices with many line items are less likely to come through with missing details.

Invoices
🧹

Cleaner invoice review rows and better category visibility

Each line in an invoice review now shows the product name and SKU together on one row. Prior-payment notes are quieter so they don't distract from new charges. Mixed invoices (bills that span categories) are now visible in every relevant reviewed category, not just one.

Review
📬

Vendor email intake gives better signals, not roadblocks

Instead of blocking an invoice when something looks unusual, the system now surfaces helpful context — whether the PO filename matches, whether a logo was recognized, which vendor is tracking the order. Error alerts are clearer and easier to act on.

Intake
📊 Charts are easier to dig into
Improvements to the data table experience on chart views.
📈

Chart data tables open in a wider, cleaner dialog

The "View data" link on charts now opens a wider table where each data row fits on a single line. Tooltip series lists in the charts themselves are also condensed so the overlay stays readable with many items.

Charts
🧭 Navigation and everyday controls feel tighter
General usability improvements across the platform.
🏷️

Components, Swag, and TikTok allocation updated on Inventory

The Components and Swag sections of Inventory have a refreshed design that matches the rest of the platform. TikTok allocation is now surfaced directly on the Inventory page.

Inventory
✨

Compact controls and text-style actions throughout the app

Controls that don't commit a change (filters, toggles, secondary actions) now appear as text links rather than boxed buttons, reducing visual clutter. All standard controls are a consistent 28px height.

UX
📥

Micro Bar business routes are now configurable from the Inbox

The routing for Micro Bar's incoming business can now be set directly in the Inbox, so emails and documents land in the right place without manual intervention.

Inbox
🐞 A few targeted fixes
Smaller corrections that remove friction from daily work.
🔢

Order tracking resolves the invoice number automatically

When tracking a labelled order, the system now picks the right invoice number on its own instead of prompting you to choose from a list.

Tracking
📋

Recipe shared-part display and quantity hints fixed

The shared-part sentence in a recipe now correctly stays grouped with the part it describes. Recipes with fractional quantities now show a hint to help with entry.

Recipes
