⭐ Big things landed this week
The three changes you'll notice right away.
🖼️

The app has a consistent frame on every page

Navigation, the top bar, and page structure are now unified across the entire platform. Every screen feels like one app, and chart colors automatically adapt to your current theme — light, dark, classic, or ledger.

App Frame
✋

"Needs You" panels surface your action items

Purchase Orders, Shipments, and Portfolio items that need your attention are now grouped in dedicated panels with inline review modals. Everything you need to make a decision is in one place, without hunting across screens.

Review
🏠

Command Center redesigned from top to bottom

The home dashboard has a fresh layout with faster time-period switching, smoother animations, revenue links that take you straight to the detail, and an activity feed filtered to just your team's people.

Command Center
🧾 Invoices work better for you this week
Cleaner workspace, more accurate data, less noise.
🎯

Attention workspace for invoices needing review

Items waiting for your action in the invoice queue now have their own workspace that pulls in the context you need — all in one focused view, with a cleaner heading and a single loading indicator.

Invoices
🔢

Statement summaries now show the right totals

Account summaries on invoice statements were showing figures that belonged to the full account, not the individual invoice. They now correctly reflect each invoice's own total and payment status.

Fix
🧹

Spend overview is cleaner, without the clutter

Unnecessary follow-up prompts and invoice-detail noise have been removed from the Spend overview. The screen now shows exactly what you need and nothing you don't.

Spend
📊 Dashboards show more and say it better
New tiles, time frame presets, and a complete activity log.
📋

Drafts tile and time frame presets on the dashboard

A Drafts tile is now pinned to the dashboard for quick access to work in progress. Common time frames — like Last 30 days — are available as presets, with All Time as the default.

Dashboard
📈

KPI tiles have labels, standing issues have more room

Key performance indicator tiles now carry clear heading labels so there's no guessing what each number means. Standing issues now show up to five items, and a market health meter sits alongside them.

Dashboard
📜

Activity history shows the complete log

The activity feed now pulls the full audit history using a proper query, so every entry is visible — not just a capped snapshot that could leave older actions out of view.

Activity
🔌 Integrations and automation got smarter
Markets, document reading, and outgoing notifications.
🔇

Pause stock alerts for markets not yet selling

You can now toggle a market as "Not selling yet" to silence its stock alerts. Handy when a market is fully set up but not yet active — no more noise until you're ready.

Markets
🤖

Invoice and packing-list reading is faster and more accurate

The AI that reads incoming invoices and packing lists was upgraded to a more capable model, improving extraction accuracy across all document types.

AI
📧

Tracking emails have a cleaner subject line

Outgoing tracking notification emails now use a straightforward subject format without the old pipe separator, making them easier to read and search in any email client.

Emails
🧭 The app is easier to navigate and recognize
New themes, a new favicon, and a panel behavior fix.
🎨

Ledger theme is now available

A new Ledger appearance joins Light, Dark, and Classic. All four themes are fully supported throughout the app, including the chart colors that now follow whichever appearance you have selected.

Theme
🔖

Browser tab shows "MWAH ERP" with a custom icon

The app's browser tab now reads "MWAH™ ERP" and displays a light-blue square favicon, making it easy to spot among a row of open tabs.

Experience
🧱

Panel hide preferences no longer bleed between sessions

Panels no longer apply "Hide" settings that were saved before the panel was opened for the current session. Each time you open a panel to work, it starts with a clean slate.

Panels
🐞 Fixes and finishing touches
Small things that make the everyday experience smoother.
🎨

Command Center tooltips match the app theme

Tooltips on top-bar icons in the Command Center now follow the active theme. The "Needs action" row was also tidied up so items display cleanly at any screen width.

Polish
🔁

No more repeated margin explanations in split view

When a split in the Command Center showed a blocked margin reason, the explanation was appearing twice. It now shows just once, where it belongs.

Fix
⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • A scoring system was added to measure how accurately the AI reads incoming invoice documents — helping the team improve extraction quality over time.
  • Multiple security and infrastructure dependencies were updated across the platform.
  • Internal build and deployment tooling was made faster and more efficient.
