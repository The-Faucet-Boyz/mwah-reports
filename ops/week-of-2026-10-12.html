⭐ The biggest things that landed this week
These are the changes the whole team will feel most.
🔌

Supplier Portal is live

Suppliers now have their own dedicated portal page to review shipments, upload tracking numbers, and see invoice details — no manual back-and-forth needed. Owners can preview exactly what a supplier sees before sharing access.

Vendor Portal
📋

Order Center board and timeline

A new operational board for purchasing gives the team a clear view of open orders, their status, and a full activity timeline — everything in one place instead of scattered across pages.

Order Center
🚛

Multi-leg freight and per-leg landed costs

Invoices now support shipments that move through multiple carriers, each leg with its own cost tracked separately. Landed cost figures stay accurate from origin to final delivery.

Invoices
🔌 The vendor portal got several new capabilities this week
New tools for staying in sync with suppliers.
👁️

View as vendor

Owners can now open a read-only preview of the supplier portal exactly as a supplier would see it — useful for setup, troubleshooting, and confirming what a supplier can access before they log in.

Vendor Portal
📦

Tracking uploads on each invoice card

Tracking information can now be uploaded directly from each invoice card in the portal. The standalone upload button has been removed to keep the page clean and the action close to the right row.

Vendor Portal
🔍

Invoice card details popover

Each invoice card in the portal now shows a details popover on the pieces line so the full line breakdown is a single click away — no need to navigate away to check counts.

Vendor Portal
🧾 Invoice and expense handling expanded this week
More flexibility in how costs are captured and attributed.
🏢

Company-wide attribution on invoice lines

Individual invoice lines can now be attributed to the whole company, not just a specific brand or team. Shared costs no longer need to be split or workaround-assigned.

Invoices
🚚

Freight allocation without stock conversion

Freight costs can now be allocated even when an invoice doesn't include a stock unit conversion step, removing a common blocker in multi-vendor receiving workflows.

Invoices
📍

WA and CA invoice uploads

Invoice uploads now handle state-specific product catalogs for Washington and California, with correct item matching for each. Teams operating in both states can upload without manual corrections.

Invoices
📊 Dashboards and purchasing summaries got clearer
Better information at a glance.
📈

Procurement KPI cards updated

The procurement summary cards now show matched invoice pricing alongside order data, giving a more complete picture of what was ordered versus what was actually invoiced.

Purchasing
📄

PO contents more readable

Purchase order line items and unit names have been simplified so they're faster to scan. The layout now focuses on what matters without extra labels cluttering each row.

Purchasing
🧭 Navigation and purchasing workflows feel smoother
Less friction day to day.
🔔

Notifications moved to the top bar

The Attention side column is replaced by a bell icon in the top navigation. Alerts appear in a popover when you need them, keeping the main workspace clear the rest of the time.

Navigation
🧃

Packaging POs: autosave and Ready status

Packaging purchase orders with flavor variants now autosave as you work and gain a Ready status indicator. Progress is preserved between sessions without a manual save step.

Purchasing
⚡

PO shipping auto-match and status filters

Purchase orders now auto-match header-level shipping by value and show invoice suggestions directly on the PO page. A new status filter with color coding makes it faster to find orders at any stage.

Purchasing
🐞 Several rough edges were smoothed out this week
Things that were slightly off and are now right.
✅

Order Center polish

This month's orders now appear first, packaging parts show up in the right spot, and the draft rail works as expected. Several small inconsistencies from the initial launch are resolved.

Order Center
📜

Vendor preview scrolls fully

The View as vendor preview previously stopped rendering at the first screen. It now scrolls through the complete supplier page so the preview is a genuine representation of the full experience.

Vendor Portal
📭

Shipments queue empty state

Clicking a stage tile on the shipments board now shows a clear "none yet" message when there are no matching items, instead of a blank space that looked like a loading error.

Shipments

  • Several library dependency updates to keep the platform current and secure.
