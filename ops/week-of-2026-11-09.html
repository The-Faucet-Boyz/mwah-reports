The Order Center board fills the screen the right way

The purchasing board now takes up the full viewport below the header, with each month column scrolling on its own — no more blank space below a tall column you can't even see. And instead of a spinner while the board loads, you now see the board's shape right away.

Pages across the platform show their layout while loading

Rather than going blank and then appearing all at once, workspaces across the app now show a skeleton of their own structure while data is on the way. Navigation clicks feel instant from the start.
