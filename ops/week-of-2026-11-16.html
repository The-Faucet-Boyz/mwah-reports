📋

The Order Center board fills the screen the right way

The purchasing board now takes up the full viewport below the header, with each month column scrolling on its own — no more blank space below a tall column you can't even see. And instead of a spinner while the board loads, you now see the board's shape right away.

Order Center
⚡

Pages across the platform show their layout while loading

Rather than going blank and then appearing all at once, workspaces across the app now show a skeleton of their own structure while data is on the way. Navigation clicks feel instant from the start.

Experience ========
🏪

Vendors page is completely rebuilt

Every vendor now has a clean table layout with status badges, a details panel that opens right on the page, and inline forms for adding records. A new Shipping tab surfaces all the relevant links in one place, and a dedicated preview tab lets you browse vendor documents without leaving the page. Receiving locations on shipment tracking forms are now clearly labeled as MWAH's own receiving places.

Vendors
📧

Purchase orders can now be emailed to vendors directly

Once a purchase order is ready, Chronicle can send it to the vendor by email straight from the platform. No more copy-paste or switching to a separate app. The order goes out through the same integrated channel the team already uses for procurement.

Procurement
🔌 Integrations got smarter this week
Procurement and invoice intake are more automated.
🧹

Invoice intake ignores irrelevant vendor replies

When a vendor replies to a purchase order thread with a message that has no attached document, the system now skips it cleanly instead of treating it as a potential invoice. Keeps the intake queue focused on real documents.

Intake
🧭 Order Center is cleaner and faster to use
Less clutter in the Order Center workflow.
📋

One order per line, one page to scroll

Orders in the Order Center now display one line each in a single continuous list. The whole page scrolls together instead of each section having its own scroll, which makes it easier to scan and navigate a large set of orders.

Order Center
➕

One New Order button, wherever you are

Previously there was a separate New Order button for each month group. Now there is a single button at the top. Easier to find, less visual noise.

Order Center
🐞 Fixes and polish landed too
A handful of issues resolved and rough edges smoothed.
🔧

Vendor portal shows the right orders now

The vendor-facing portal was incorrectly showing empty lanes when orders existed, and hiding some orders that belong to merged vendor accounts. Both are fixed — vendors now see the accurate picture of their open and completed orders.
