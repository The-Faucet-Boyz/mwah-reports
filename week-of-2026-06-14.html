⭐ Highlights of the week
🎨

The CRM got a visual overhaul — new typeface, redesigned charts, and animated load-ins across the whole app

A full design audit wave landed this week. The CRM now uses the Archivo typeface throughout, giving cards, tables, and numbers a cleaner, more consistent look. The Insights and Trends charts were redesigned with a single accent color and lighter gridlines — easier to read, cleaner in both light and dark mode. Numbers on KPI cards now animate in on load, and those same smooth animations rolled out to Insights, Trends, All Orders, Team Performance, and the Activity tabs. The dashboard's KPI layout was also re-weighted so the headline number (Revenue, Commission, Orders) leads, with supporting details stepping back — same information, just in the right order. Month-over-month swings now show a ▲ or ▼ arrow and only color up when the move is worth noticing.

Visual refresh
🧪

Buyers can now request trade samples right from the shop — and you get a Slack ping the moment one lands

A "Pick your samples." card now lives in the buyer portal's shop — buyers choose up to 10 of each flavor, confirm, and the request rides their order as its own separate MWAH-S PO. Your team gets a Slack #fulfillment ping and an in-app notification, so nothing falls through the cracks. Confirming the PO approves the request; declining it (with a reason) shows the buyer a "Declined" message along with your note. Portal Mgmt → Settings has a new Trade Samples section with a master on/off switch, per-strain availability, per-request and per-month caps, and a custom pause message — all enforced server-side at submit.

Buyer portal
🛍️ Buyer portal
❓

New "Help & Support" menu in the portal nav

Buyers now have a "? Help & Support" dropdown between Account and Cart with three options — Knowledge base, Contact us (opens Intercom chat), and Submit a request. Mobile buyers get the same three items in their menu.

Buyer portal
📝

Submit a request now opens a real form, not a blank chat

Tapping "Submit a request" opens a quick Bug / Feature / Other form with a title and description. Requests arrive in a dedicated Slack channel for the team to triage instead of going to the general chat.

Buyer portal
🛒

Cart no longer refills after a buyer empties it

Removing items from the cart now sticks across page reloads. Previously, clearing the cart and coming back to the shop would repopulate it with the removed items.

Bug fix
✏️

Change quantities or remove items right on the Review page

Buyers can now adjust quantities and remove items directly on the Review order screen instead of having to go back to Shop to make a change.

Buyer portal
💰

Minimum order total enforced at checkout

When a buyer's cart is under the minimum your team set in Portal Settings, the Shop and Review screens show exactly how much more to add — and the order can't be submitted until the minimum is met.

Portal settings
🔄

Stale portal tabs now recover on their own

A buyer tab left open for a long time that hit "could not read your account data" now quietly refreshes once to pick up the latest version and lands you signed in, instead of staying stuck.

"Submit a request" entries from the portal now land in a dedicated #buyer-feedback channel instead of the shared #fulfillment firehose, so ops can find them without wading through order activity.

Portal mgmt

"View as buyer" is now available to all CRM roles

Field and inside reps can now open the buyer portal in impersonation mode to help a buyer with their order — not just managers. The session stays read-only and audit-logged.

📦 Orders + fulfillment
🔗

GrowFlow reconciliation now catches everything as volume grows

The daily sync now recognizes GrowFlow's real "closed" transfer stage and no longer caps the fetch at 200 transfers per day — so completed deliveries reconcile reliably no matter how busy the week gets.

Bug fix
✉️

Editing a delivery contact no longer loses your place

On the order detail page, editing the delivery contact no longer resets focus or causes the page to flicker while it refreshes in the background. The Edit option is also hidden once an order is delivered or cancelled.

Bug fix
🛡️

CSV exports are now safe to open in Excel and Sheets

Any cell that starts with a special character that Excel or Google Sheets would try to run as a formula is now automatically neutralized — so exported data opens as plain text, not executable content.

Security
📱 Dashboard + CRM
✨

Dashboard comes to life on load — and pills show you what's behind the count

KPI cards ease in and numbers count up from zero on first load. Hover any "Needs Attention" pill (overdue, unread, change requests, drafts) to preview the actual items behind the number — no more clicking blind.

Dashboard
📱

Mobile Stores list now uses a proper filter sheet

The filter chips that used to stack above the store list on mobile are now tucked behind a single "Filters" button that opens a full-screen sheet — so the first store is visible without scrolling past three rows of filters.

Mobile

Deleting an activity, task, or contact now shows an Undo toast. The confirm dialog also no longer says the action is permanent — because these deletes have always been reversible, and now it's honest about that.

Bug fix

"Overdue" and "due today" pills now open your Calendar

Clicking the overdue or due-today pill in the "Needs Attention" bar on the dashboard now navigates to your Calendar, and hovering any pill shows the full task name instead of cutting it off.

A person can now carry multiple email addresses, managed as pills in the edit form — pick a primary, add more, remove old ones. The contact edit form also now dims the page behind it, matching how stores are edited.
