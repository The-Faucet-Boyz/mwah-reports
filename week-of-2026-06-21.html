⭐ Highlights of the week
💳

Store credit has its own home — a full Credits dashboard for managers

Managers and admins now have a dedicated Credits page under Orders. It shows the total outstanding balance across all stores, lifetime issued vs. applied, a chart of how old open credits are (so you can chase the ones sitting longest), and a per-store drill-down with the complete history of every credit. All Orders totals now reflect any applied store credit too, matching what's shown in the order detail.

New feature
📦

Inventory tracking is on its way — the new Inventory page is here with demo data to explore

A new Inventory page landed in the sidebar this week, building out in stages. By week's end it has three tabs — Store Triage (stock-status vials per store), By Product (units in field, sell-thru, days supply, stores running low), and Reorder Queue (proposals grouped by status) — all populated with a realistic demo dataset so you can feel the layout before live POSaBIT data is connected. A clear "DEMO DATA" banner makes it obvious while the sync is still being wired up.

Coming soon
🔗 Reconciliation
🚫

Mark labs and processors as "Not relevant" — they'll never come back

Non-retail counterparties like labs and processors (H&M Extracts, Confidence Analytics, etc.) can now be marked "Not relevant" once. Every future sync skips them automatically, and a manager can un-ignore from a dedicated list if needed.

Reconciliation
✅

Resolved decisions stay resolved — no more re-appearing mismatches

A license mismatch you've already resolved no longer re-flags itself on the next sync. The recon engine now recognizes a store's full license history (renewals and successions), and resolved decisions clear out of the Decisions tab and the dashboard "needs attention" count.

Bug fix
🔄

Clearer license chooser with a new "Convert to renewal" option

The license-mismatch chooser now leads with the in-place renewal path (the common case), explains the difference between renewal and succession, and steers you to a merge when the GrowFlow license already belongs to another store. Admins can also convert a succession back to a same-store renewal if it was logged incorrectly.

Reconciliation
📝

Add notes to any past license change from the Licenses tab

Admins can now attach a note to any historical license change — renewals, corrections, and successions — explaining who changed it and why. The context is visible right from Store Detail without digging through logs.

Store detail
📦 Orders + fulfillment
💰

~$39.9K of May/June revenue recovered in the Monthly Sales export

A bug was silently dropping transferred orders that were missing a GrowFlow sale date from the export. The fix correctly windows by recognized-revenue date, recovering the affected orders — and a one-time backfill stamped the date on all historical orders that were already impacted.

Bug fix
🔒

Lock a finished fulfillment week with one click

A new "Week Complete" toggle on each fulfillment week lets you close out a finished cycle. New orders route to the next week automatically, and any orders still needing checks can be rolled forward in a single click. The locked week stays fully visible and editable — and you can reopen it anytime.

Fulfillment
⚡

Portal document downloads are dramatically faster

Fetching a portal document was consistently taking 4.5–6 seconds and tripping a performance alert. Parallelizing the work cuts that down to a fraction — the file link now appears almost immediately.

Performance
🔗

Fulfillment Slack alerts now link straight to the PO or store

Order and store alerts (new portal order, confirmed, cancelled, trade-sample request, license correction, and more) now carry a direct link to open that exact PO or store in the CRM — no more hunting through the list. New portal orders also tag Andy and Luu directly.

Fulfillment alerts
📱 Dashboard + CRM
🔔

Customize exactly which notifications reach your bell

A new Notification Settings panel in your avatar menu lets you turn off the types you don't want cluttering your feed — stale-order nudges, birthdays, and more. You can also set noisy types to "Group," collapsing them into a single tidy row you can expand when you want the detail.

Notifications
🗺️

See a whole chain on the map — and a radius ring around them

The map's new chain picker plots every location in a chain together, and a 1/2/5-mile radius toggle highlights nearby stores. Clear it anytime to return to the full map. Handy for planning routes and spotting expansion targets near your accounts.

Map
⚡

Log the same activity across multiple stores at once

Hit "Duplicate" on any activity in the feed to reopen the log form pre-filled with its details — tweak and save as a new entry. While duplicating you can also choose "log to more stores" to record the same visit or call across several stores without re-entering anything.

Activities

All Orders: click any row to open its PO, and credits show a badge

Every order row in All Orders now opens its PO detail on click (some rows used to do nothing). Orders with a store credit applied show a small "CR" badge on the total — hover it to see the credit amount and original price.

Orders

Samples tab in Store Detail is redesigned and cleaner

Trade samples now collapse into cards grouped by PO, and direct samples group by day — instead of one row per flavor. "GrowFlow" samples are now labeled "Trade" to match how they're talked about. Sample POs have also moved off the Orders tab and live only here.

Store detail
📅

Calendar: "Vendor Day" replaces the rarely-used "Holiday" event type

The calendar event type picker now has a Vendor Day option instead of Holiday — a better fit for the events the field team actually needs to log.

Calendar

Granting portal access now auto-links the store to their CRM contact

When you grant a buyer portal access to a store, their CRM contact is automatically linked to that store — they appear under Linked Stores, and the buyer becomes a selectable contact in portal checkout. Removing access leaves the CRM link in place.

The Visit Prep export now includes columns for Website, Owners, Buyers, and Budtenders, plus a Portal Ordering column showing whether the store orders through the portal. Preferred Delivery Day lives under a renamed "Fulfillment" section alongside Fulfillment Notes.

Login: "Show password" and "Keep me signed in" — now in one row

The login screen has a "Show password" checkbox so you can verify what you typed before signing in. It now sits side-by-side with "Keep me signed in" just above the Sign In button, on both the CRM and the buyer portal.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • A crash when opening the New Contest form (a stale event handler throwing a ReferenceError on load) is fixed — the form wires up cleanly every time.
  • Monthly Sales export backfill: all historical transferred orders that were missing a GrowFlow sale date have been stamped — no more gaps in future exports.
  • Fulfillment frozen columns: a hairline see-through seam that could appear between the frozen first columns at certain zoom levels or on HiDPI displays is closed.
  • Browser-extension "Tab not found" errors are filtered from Sentry so they stop generating un-actionable noise issues.
  • Reconciliation "Not relevant" counterparties list is now collapsible and paginated at 25 per page — a long ignore list stays manageable.
  • Portal ordering and trade-sample pause/resume now send a Slack ping to #all-mwah, so an accidental kill-switch flip gets caught and reversed fast.
  • Menu Intelligence foundation: a new back-end capability is being built to track MWAH's position on dispensary menus (Dutchie, Weedmaps, Leafly, Jane) and understand competitive pricing over time — early data collection wiring landed this week.
  • Dependencies updated: commander (MCP CLI), vitest (test runner), and Sentry SDK all bumped to latest. Vite security patch applied on the website.
  • Website: Cloudflare HTML-cache decision documented — HTML stays uncached at the edge until an automated purge step exists, to avoid stale compliance copy after updates.
