⭐ Highlights of the week
🎛️

Make the dashboard yours — drag, pin, save, and switch between custom views

Your dashboard is now fully customizable. A new Customize button lets you drag widgets into any order, pin the ones you care about, hide the ones you don't, and pull in more from a widget library. When you get your layout right, save it with a name — "Monday pack prep," "Month-end review," whatever — and switch between saved views from a new Views menu. Your setup follows you across devices and updates the moment a new order comes in.

Dashboard
🔄

VMI is end-to-end — proposals, auto-approval, warehouse stock, buyer self-service, and chain group orders all live

This week completed the full VMI loop. Reps can build a reorder proposal from the Inventory page, send it with a personal note in a pre-send compose window, and let the system auto-place if the store doesn't respond in time (with a Decline link in the email for stores that don't want autopilot). Buyers manage their own settings and see their live shelf from the portal. Chains get one group email covering every low-stock location. And a new Warehouse tab shows MWAH's own GrowFlow stock — on hand, reserved, and sellable — so reps know what's available to ship before they build a proposal.

🎛️ Dashboard
📌

Save named dashboard views and switch on the fly

Save your current layout as a named view and switch between views from the Views menu — so your "start of day" setup and "end of week" setup can both exist without one overwriting the other.

New feature
⚡

Dashboard numbers refresh in real time

The dashboard now updates the moment a new order comes in or an order is transferred — no page reload needed. Pipeline totals stay locked to the active pipeline even when the month rolls over.

Bug fix
🏷️

Top Drivers now labels new stores clearly

The Top Drivers widget labels its change column "MoM" and marks brand-new stores as "New" — so a big jump from zero doesn't look like a sudden surge from an existing account.

Polish
📦 Inventory + VMI
🏗️

Warehouse tab shows MWAH's own GrowFlow stock

A new Warehouse tab on the Inventory page shows every MWAH lot in the WA warehouse — units on hand, reserved, and sellable per flavor, plus which lots are expiring soon. Only MWAH products and bulk Distillate show; other brands sharing our GrowFlow account are filtered out with a small note.

New feature
📧

One-tap reorder emails — buyers confirm right from the inbox

Buyers now tap "Confirm order" directly inside the proposal email and the PO is drafted without a separate review page. They can still tap "Edit this order" to adjust before confirming — the one-tap path is the shortcut, not a lock-in.

VMI
⏱️

Stores that don't respond get auto-placed

VMI stores that don't reply to a reorder proposal by the deadline now have their order placed automatically. The proposal email says so up front and includes a Decline link. Stores that want to click every time can switch to "Require explicit approval" in their VMI settings.

VMI
🔗

Chain group orders: pick which locations to include

When sending a chain's group reorder, each store now has a checkbox — you can deselect any locations you're skipping this cycle. The totals, email, and created orders all follow your selection.

VMI
📋

Inventory loads fast at scale, reorder queue is smarter

The Inventory page now shows the first batch of stores immediately and loads the rest as you scroll — no more slow full-page loads with 177+ stores. Stores that were just delivered are held out of the reorder queue until their stock numbers catch up, so you don't get nudged to reorder a shop that was restocked yesterday.

Performance
❌

Cancel an open VMI proposal

Reps can now cancel a proposal that's out but shouldn't be — it withdraws the proposal, instantly disables the buyer's approval link, and marks the row "Cancelled" in the Proposals tab.

VMI
🛍️ Buyer portal
🛒

Abandoned cart reminders go out automatically

Buyers who save a cart in the portal but don't submit it get a friendly reminder email two days later. They can turn reminders off anytime under Account > Profile.

New feature
📊

VMI self-service: buyers manage their own settings in the portal

VMI-enrolled stores can review and update their approval window and supply targets from the portal, and changes sync back to the CRM in real time. Stores not yet on VMI see a clear explainer and a quick way to share their register info.

Portal Mgmt is down to 5 clear tabs

The Portal Mgmt page has been reorganized from 11 tabs into 5: Dashboard, Directory (Stores + Users), Inbox (all 5 buyer queues with counts), Health (Email + Performance), and Settings. The Abandoned Carts queue now works, the Stores search is wired up, and the whole page fits on a phone screen.

Overhaul
🗑️

Abandoned Carts queue for staff

Portal Mgmt has a new Abandoned Carts view — saved buyer carts that never became orders, with the cart snapshot, buyer contact, and estimated value. Reps can add a note or close a row with a reason; rows auto-close when the buyer orders.

New feature
📦 Orders + fulfillment
🚚

GrowFlow delivery status is now in the PO

The PO detail panel has a Shipping card showing the GrowFlow manifest timeline — departed, arrived, and scheduled delivery. Fulfillment rows show an "In transit" or "Arrived" chip at a glance, and managers can toggle on payment status to see which invoices are paid.

New feature
💰

Price Groups — track per-store deals in the CRM

Set a per-store or per-chain deal ($/unit off, per-product overrides) under Admin › Price Groups. Orders and invoices stay at full list price, but your dashboard commission and net sales now reflect the real economics. All Orders and Fulfillment rows show a small deal badge — the rate is kept internal.

New feature

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Fulfillment schedule cutoffs (12pm Tue/Fri → Mon–Wed / Wed–Fri cycles) are now editable in the admin panel instead of hardcoded — the schedule can be adjusted without a code change.
  • Admin panel was reorganized: 10 tabs (Orders/Data grouping), role definitions explained inline, data-wipe tools grouped under Reset Data, and mobile user rows expand to show Disable/Mark-internal actions.
  • The MCP CLI got a correctness and parity audit — stale tool descriptions, off-by-one date windows, and coverage gaps were fixed; drift guards now run in the local pre-commit hook instead of CI.
  • GrowFlow BQ extension instance was renamed to fit the 63-character Cloud Functions name limit — had been silently failing scheduled syncs.
  • VMI proposals no longer create a draft order on send — a holder waits for buyer approval, then the draft is created; no more orphan drafts from proposals nobody confirmed.
  • The ops-sync API gained a `/v1/growflow-inventory` endpoint and the warehouse-sync cadence became a config value so it can be tuned without a redeploy.
  • Automation tooling was updated: standup, mock, and ship skills were tuned; a timezone-reviewer subagent was added to catch day-bucketing issues at review time.
  • Team Performance scorecard was retired from the Reports menu — performance data still lives on the dashboard and Activity page, which is where managers already use it.
  • CA locator: Nabis pagination was fixed (orders endpoint corrected, 0-indexed pagination), so all CA stores now import across every page — not just the first.
