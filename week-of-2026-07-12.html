⭐ Highlights of the week
🚨

My Scorecard and Sales Coach were showing 0 for every rep since March — now fixed

A silent bug had been zeroing out visits, calls, and emails on every rep's personal scorecard, the Team Scorecard, and the Sales Coach widget since March 2026. The numbers looked wrong but never errored, so the issue went unnoticed for over four months. This week's deep design audit caught it — the data was always there, it just wasn't being read correctly. All three surfaces now reflect actual activity.

Critical fix
📊

Insights and Trends got a full accuracy pass — labels, charts, and time windows all corrected

A thorough audit of the Insights, Trends, and Competitor Pricing pages found and fixed several issues reps had probably just learned to work around: the "Prospect" KPI now clearly says "Never ordered · has buying power" so it doesn't get confused with the broader Store Health label; the sell-through trend chart no longer shows a trendline through the current incomplete day; month-over-month comparison shows "—" instead of a fake $0 when history doesn't go back far enough; and a dead "vs prior period" checkbox that never did anything was removed. The pages now say what they mean.

Accuracy
🎛️ Dashboard
📈

Revenue trend bars look right again

The projected current-month bar was sometimes rendering shorter than smaller prior months — it looked like revenue was declining when it wasn't. Fixed so the bar accurately shows how the month is tracking relative to history.

Bug fix
🎯

KPI cards are easier to read at a glance

KPI icons are now color-coded, foot metrics are in bold black, and delta indicators (the up/down arrows) now light up at a 1% change instead of 5% — so small meaningful moves register earlier.

Polish
🔔

Portal and Inventory badges show the moment you log in

The notification badges on the Portal Mgmt and Inventory sidebar links now load at startup, so you can see pending items and attention alerts before you visit those pages. People QR sign-up counts also appear on the People nav icon.

New feature
📊 Analytics
🧪

Samples Given Out chart no longer double-counts

The Samples chart was counting samples twice — once from CRM activity logs and once from GrowFlow transaction data. It now counts only CRM-logged samples, which is the accurate number your reps actually recorded.

Bug fix
📉

Sell-through trend is honest about the current period

The trendline on the sell-through chart was extending through today's in-progress day, which artificially pulled the line down mid-week. It now stops at the most recent complete day so the trend reflects actual finished data.

Bug fix
🏪

Chain Locations status is now accurate

The Status column in a chain's Locations tab was showing "Active" for every store regardless of actual buying recency. It now derives the right status — buying, overdue, or new — the same way the rest of the CRM does.

Bug fix
🛍️ Buyer portal
📡

Portal analytics pilot — understanding how buyers actually use the portal

A privacy-first analytics pilot launched on the buyer portal this week. It tracks what buyers click on and where they drop off — no personal data, no order details — just enough signal to understand which parts of the portal work well and which need improvement. The team uses the data; buyers' privacy is protected.

New feature
👥 Portal management
🏷️

Price Groups moved to the Stores tab

Per-store deal tracking (Price Groups) now lives under the Stores tab in Admin where it makes more sense alongside store-specific settings. Dark-mode contrast on the pending buyer license panel was also fixed.

Polish
🧢

Monthly trade sample cap — now admin-configurable

Admins can now set a company-wide monthly cap on how many trade samples go out through the portal. When the cap is reached, the portal automatically pauses sample ordering for the rest of the month. Reps handing out samples directly are not affected — only portal trade samples count.

New feature
📦 Orders + fulfillment
🔍

Contact merge tool is safer and more transparent

Two bugs were fixed in the Find Duplicates merge flow: an excluded contact could previously slip through and win the merge (now blocked at the selection step), and errors during merge were silently swallowed — you'd see a success toast even if part of the merge failed. Failures are now surfaced clearly.

Bug fix
🎨 Visual & design
🖋️

Notification rule builder redesigned

The admin panel's Notification Rules builder got a visual overhaul — cleaner header, grouped steps with a proper sub-text, and a preview card that shows what the rule will actually send before you save it.

Redesign
✅

GrowFlow API Review panel is cleaner and clearer

The reconciliation "GrowFlow API Review" tab was redesigned to use the CRM's standard visual language — blocking vs. data-quality items are now distinguished by a small status dot on the section header rather than color-coded side bars, and the layout is consistent in both light and dark mode.

Redesign
🌙

Dark mode polish across 10+ surfaces

A sweep this week fixed dark-mode contrast, spacing, and alignment across tables, reconciliation tabs, the samples table, and several card components. The app should look consistently polished in dark mode now, not just light.

Polish
🏭

Inventory warehouse table shows more useful columns

The lot table in the Warehouse tab now shows Harvested date and Oldest stock instead of Expiration and Batch — the latter were always blank for MWAH goods. The layout and column alignment were also cleaned up, and the always-zero "Lots expiring" KPI was removed.

Polish
🌐 Public website — gimmemwah.com
🗺️

Find Us map and store list stay in sync as you explore

The store list on the Find Us page now updates automatically as you pan and zoom the map — you only see the stores that are currently in view. No more scrolling through a full list when you're zoomed into a neighborhood.

Improvement
📱

Mobile locator experience improved

Several mobile usability improvements landed on the public site: the product carousel has clearer navigation cues, the locator is smoother to scroll on small screens, and the overall mobile feel is tighter.

Polish
⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • The in-app "What's New" modal is gone — it was rarely opened. Version tracking continues; reps just won't be interrupted by a pop-up.
  • The public website's store locator was accidentally exposing internal CRM store IDs in its data feed — these were removed and the feed was hardened.
  • Activity icons had a mismatch since the order-type normalization change — orders were showing the phone (call) icon. Fixed.
  • Calendar month-view was rendering store names without escaping them — a potential XSS vector caught and closed in the design audit.
  • GrowFlow warehouse mapping was incorrectly promoting 30,000 grams of bulk oil as 30k units of finished sellable product. The mapping now correctly ignores raw materials and non-sellable lots.
  • The menu intelligence endpoint was 500ing in production due to a missing database index — fixed with a new pointer-doc architecture that also makes the query much faster and complete across all stores.
  • CI security audit now enforces dependency vulnerability scanning — failures are no longer silently ignored.
  • Mobile reorder carousel got 44px touch targets on its nav arrows, meeting accessibility sizing standards.
  • The revenue projection formula is now one shared helper instead of four independent copies — so all the surfaces that show "on pace for" will always agree.
  • Ops platform parity tests now pin the BQ view queries to code so a view drift can't silently break the API.
