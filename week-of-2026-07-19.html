⭐ Highlights of the week
📦

The VMI reorder flow got a top-to-bottom rebuild this week

Three interconnected pieces of the vendor-managed reorder system all shipped together. The purchase order reps send to ops now looks right — MWAH logo, proper two-column layout, correct quantities. The adjustment window and delivery date pickers moved onto the reorder builder itself, so every quantity and coverage line updates live as you change them. And the suggested reorder quantities now factor in the actual delivery schedule, not just raw stock. What used to take multiple back-and-forths to square up is now one coherent form that produces a polished PO in one pass.

New feature
🔭

Competitor pricing tracking cut its error rate from 52% to near zero

The Menu Intelligence system that tracks competitor pricing at dispensaries was quietly failing on over half of all store checks. The root cause: roughly 130 stores in the crawl list had no online menu to pull from — they were never going to return data. The system now detects menuless stores on first check and skips them for 28 days, focusing crawl time on stores where pricing data actually exists. Competitor pricing on the Insights page should now be reliably up to date across all relevant stores.

Major fix
📦 Inventory & reorders
🍓

Pipeline now shows how many units of each flavor are committed

A new Flavor Count tab in Pipeline rolls up units of each MWAH flavor across all active orders — so you can watch inventory draw-down in real time without opening every order individually. Samples are included since they pull from the same physical stock.

New feature
🏭

Warehouse "Sellable" count no longer includes raw distillate

The Sellable quantity in the Warehouse tab was counting bulk distillate as finished sellable product — inflating the available number. It now excludes non-sellable lots, so the count reflects actual units that can ship.

Bug fix
📅

Reorder suggestions now fit the actual delivery schedule

Suggested reorder quantities are now calculated against the real delivery window, not just raw stock levels. What gets suggested will arrive when there's actually a delivery to attach it to.

Improvement
🗓️ Calendar & dashboard
🔴

Calendar shows a live "now" line as you work

Day and week views now show a red current-time indicator — a thin horizontal rule with a dot — positioned at the exact current minute. In week view it's pinned to today's column so it doesn't float across the whole grid. The line moves as the day progresses.

New feature
⚡

Switching dashboard views is noticeably faster

The Views dropdown was rebuilding the entire dashboard every time it opened, even when you weren't switching views. It now only rebuilds when the view actually changes, so toggling between your saved views is instant.

Performance
🛍️ Buyer portal
📥

Portal inbox is clearer and more actionable

The portal inbox got a cleanup this week: timestamps now show correctly, the rejection flow has clearer labels and a more prominent button, and Slack alerts fire at the right moments so the team doesn't miss incoming requests.

Polish

When a buyer hits an error during portal checkout, it now pings the ops team right away — so problems get investigated quickly instead of sitting in a log file until someone thinks to look.

Improvement
📊 Competitor pricing
🏪

Jane Dispensary locations now show up in competitor data

The competitor pricing tracker was missing Jane stores in the crawl — a mapping gap that's now fixed. Jane's current pricing is captured and shows alongside other competitors in the Insights view.

Bug fix

Different dispensary menu systems use different category names for the same product types, which made the competitor pricing table hard to read. Categories are now normalized so the same products group together across all stores.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Every browser confirm/alert popup in the CRM is now a branded, centered dialog — no more Chrome's ugly URL-bar chip style on deletions and confirmations. 80 sites across the app converted.
  • Chain store counts and KPI numbers now stay in sync across every surface — a drift where the KPI tab and Orders tab showed different store totals was fixed.
  • JS and CSS files can now be cached permanently in your browser — each deploy gets a unique stamp, so pages load faster on repeat visits without serving stale code.
  • Sentry's Firestore error queue now self-heals when it falls over — errors surface cleanly and with full context instead of getting dropped.
  • New end-to-end automated tests cover the full portal checkout flow and CRM order money paths — so regressions in the most critical flows get caught before they ship.
  • Reconciliation bulk-action buttons are centered again, and the card layout in light mode no longer shows white text on a white background.
  • Store license succession ("Convert to renewal") now shows a loading spinner during the conversion, and handles edge cases that used to silently fail.
  • Several upcoming MWAH flavors are now registered in the product catalog ahead of launch — the system is ready when they go live.
  • Slack: fulfillment leads are no longer tagged on every Order Confirmed notification — only order-specific fulfillment alerts fire to the right people.
  • Competitor pricing data deduplication is now enforced — the same store's prices can't appear twice in an Insights comparison.
