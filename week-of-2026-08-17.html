⭐ Highlights of the week
🔔

The Alert Center got a major upgrade

Alerts are now smarter in every direction. When the same issue fires a burst of pings, they get coalesced into one clean notification instead of flooding the channel. A new availability alert type fires when something critical stops working. Alerts now tag the right person directly at dispatch time based on who's on duty. And specific alert types can be given a skip code so they quiet down without drowning out the ones that matter.

New feature
🍵

Two new flavors are live on the website — with campaign film

Strawberry Matcha and Wild Watermelon Ice have launched with full dedicated pages on gimmemwah.com. The homepage shows a rotating hero feature for the new drops, and a Strawberry Matcha campaign video is live for customers to experience the brand story before they visit a store.

Brand launch
🔔 Alert Center
📧

Hannah + Maddie now get email alerts for three buyer situations

Three buyer-facing situations — the ones most likely to need immediate attention from the ops team — now trigger a direct email to Hannah and Maddie. The team can also configure which buyer alerts route to email going forward.

New feature
✅

Alert parity checks are running again

The checks that verify alert counts are accurate and consistent were silently failing. They're fixed and running, so alert numbers you see reflect what's actually happening.

Bug fix
🛍️ Buyer portal
🎬

Flavor and video engagement now tracked per product

The portal now measures which flavor pages buyers visit and whether they watch the promo video for each product. This gives the team visibility into which flavors are generating the most interest before a buyer places an order.

New tracking
🖼️

Product images on order detail are back

Flavor thumbnails on the order detail screen had stopped loading and were falling back to a blank image. They're back and pulling the correct art for each product.

Bug fix
👁️

"View as buyer" is more stable

The View-as-buyer mode had two separate issues: it was occasionally logging outages during the account handoff, and it sometimes raced with the authentication setup on load. Both are fixed.

Bug fix
✉️

Checkout email validation tightened

The checkout canary that validates orders at submission now applies the same email rules as the form itself — so the same address is accepted or rejected at both stages.

Bug fix
📦 Orders & reconciliation
🔗

The reconciliation link picker has been redesigned

When linking a settlement match in reconciliation, the picker and confirmation modals now show copy that adapts to what you're actually doing — making it much clearer what you're about to confirm before you commit.

UX improvement
🔄

Amount Review is no longer a dead end

When a settlement had a line in Amount Review, the page was getting stuck in a refresh loop with no way forward. It now reads the right evidence and surfaces a clear path to resolution.

Bug fix
🧹

Unmatched blockers don't get re-carried after manual resolution

When a blocker was already resolved out-of-band (e.g. manually linked), the system was still carrying it forward on subsequent passes as if it were unresolved. That's fixed — once it's linked, it stays linked.

Bug fix
📱

Order info shows first on mobile PO detail

On the purchase order detail screen on mobile, the Order Info panel was loading as the secondary view instead of the primary one. The read order is corrected.

Bug fix
🛒 Products & deals
🎥

Each product can now have its own promo video

In the admin panel, you can now upload a promotional video to each individual product. A "video available" badge appears on the product listing so it's clear which flavors have video assets ready for the portal.

New feature
⚠️

A warning appears before a deal window closes

When you're about to end a deal, you'll now see a confirmation prompt before it's gone — whether you accessed it through the price group screen or the store detail view. No more accidentally closing out a deal.

New feature
🗂️

Product and sample controls are now in one place

The admin views for managing products and sample allocations have been brought into one consolidated section, reducing the back-and-forth between screens when making related changes.

Improvement
📊 Dashboard & notifications
🧮

Monthly sample costs now show the real count

The monthly costs KPI on the dashboard was undercounting samples — it wasn't picking up all real sample lines. It now reflects the accurate total for the month.

Bug fix
🔕

Test-store orders no longer trigger change-request pings

The sweep that checks for stale pending change requests was pinging for orders attached to test stores — which don't represent real accounts. Those false positives are filtered out.

Bug fix
💬

Sample-limit tooltips are clearer in the admin panel

The explainers for per-account sample limits in the admin panel have been converted from inline warning banners to cleaner info tooltips, and the workspace heading now uses the correct font.

Improvement
📦 Inventory (POSaBIT)
🔗

Inventory stays linked when a store's identity changes

When a store's identity information changed in the CRM (license renewal, correction, etc.), the corresponding POSaBIT mapping was sometimes coming loose. The two are now kept in sync automatically.

Bug fix
