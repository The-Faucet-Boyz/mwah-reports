⭐ Highlights
📦

Inventory finally tells the whole story — four CRM releases in seven days, all converging on proposals and counts

This was the biggest single-week Inventory overhaul in the CRM's history. Tab counts now show real work items rather than raw send totals. Proposals for products a store has already ordered past no longer appear as things to resend. A new Proposal Metrics tab surfaces trend data so reps can see whether a product is gaining traction before committing to another send cycle. And the entire Inventory area now shares one consistent design across all five tabs.

Product detail pages on gimmemwah.com now include a scrollable gallery wired to flavor navigation. Clicking a flavor loads its white-ground WA product renders so customers know exactly what they're picking. This is the first time flavor-specific photography has been surfaced on the public site.

🛒 Buyer portal
🔒

Portal switch gates only buyer self-serve — rep tools unaffected

Turning off the portal switch no longer affects rep-side Inventory, Market view, or low-stock emails. The switch now controls what buyers can self-serve, not what reps can see. Hidden flavors are also consistently hidden everywhere the switch applies.

Buyer portal
📈

Product performance columns are clearer, runway bars show actual numbers

The portal's Product performance view was reorganized — column headers are self-explanatory, and runway bars now show the actual number alongside the bar so buyers can read it without doing mental math.

Buyer portal
🔄

Portal order submissions are dramatically more reliable

Three separate edge-case bugs were fixed in the portal's order submission flow: a committed order now echoes back before any checkout gate re-checks, a paused retry is no longer re-offered after the server already refused it, and a lost-response retry is properly allowed through the pause gate. Orders should behave predictably in all cases now.

Portal orders
🎁

Trade sample switch respected consistently in the portal

Buyer trade samples now follow the portal on/off switch and the per-flavor sample switch is enforced. Sample orders behave consistently with non-sample orders.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Four CRM releases in seven days (v102.46 through v102.49) — the fastest release cadence this year.
  • Firebase Admin 14.4.0, React 19.3.0, resend 6.28.0, and YAML 2.9.1 bumped across CRM functions, MCP, and dev dependencies.
  • CI now auto-builds and deploys the hosted MCP server image on every merge to the MCP package directory.
  • Failed scheduled jobs on the website project now alert #infra-alerts so outages don't go silent.
  • CRM to Google Contacts sync made opt-in, preventing unintended overwrites of existing contact records.
  • POSaBIT capture timestamps corrected: the published timestamp is always recorded after the fetch, never before a row's source update time.
  • VMI low-register-data check corrected to ask the right question at the line level, not the store level.
  • Insights live data refresh no longer scrolls reps back to the top of the Overview tab.
  • Website: vitest and gitignore updated to ignore Claude worktree directories so test runs and deploys stay clean.
  • Apps Script deploy verification now checks the actual Google account identity, not just clasp config — prevents silent deploys under the wrong account.
