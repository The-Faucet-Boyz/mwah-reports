⭐ Highlights
🔔

The CRM now catches when GrowFlow quietly changes a booked order — and flags it for review before it becomes a settlement problem

When GrowFlow modifies an order that the team has already reconciled and booked, the CRM now detects that change automatically and surfaces it in the Reconciliation inbox. Reps and managers can see exactly what changed, decide how to handle it, and the system locks the row while the decision is saving so nothing gets applied twice. This closes a gap that previously let after-the-fact GrowFlow edits slip through unnoticed.

Reconciliation
📍

The store locator on gimmemwah.com got a full visual tune-up — cleaner tags, sharper status symbols, and a refined color system

Customers visiting the Find Us page now see a much cleaner map: store tags were simplified to show only what matters, the colors and status icons (SOON, INCOMING, etc.) were refined for better contrast and readability, and filter labels now stay legible at larger text sizes. This is the biggest locator polish pass since the Pearanormal launch layer went in last week.

🔄 Reconciliation
🕵️

Changed-after-booking: GrowFlow edits to booked orders are now flagged automatically

If a GrowFlow transfer comes back different from what was booked, the Reconciliation inbox now highlights the discrepancy so it can be reviewed and resolved rather than silently applied.

Reconciliation
✅

Settlement blockers now say when the problem is fixed and you're just waiting for the next sync

Previously, a resolved blocker looked the same as an active one until the next sync cleared it. Now the CRM explicitly tells you "fixed — waiting for next check" so you know no further action is needed.

Reconciliation
🔒

Decision rows now lock and show progress while saving, preventing double-applies

When you accept or reject a changed-after-booking decision, the row immediately locks and shows a saving indicator, so there's no confusion about whether the action went through.

Reconciliation

Every change to chain membership now creates an audit record. If removing a store from a chain would cause an in-progress deal to be dropped, the CRM warns you before the change is applied.

Two order-editing fixes: when a manager edits a line item, the total dollar amount now stays correct; and direct sample order rows now open their detail view on tap as expected.
