⭐ Highlights
🗺️

The CRM now has a full Market tab — WA state data, county breakdowns, and store-level trends all in one place

A brand-new Market tab is live, pulling in Washington state purchase data alongside county and individual store trends. Reps can now see how their accounts stack up against the broader market, drill into monthly history, and spot competitive patterns — all without leaving the CRM. Market cards, source labels, and navigation were also refined throughout the week as the feature rolled out.

Market Intelligence
📋

The CRM now automatically drafts restock proposals for stores not on VMI

For stores that aren't managed through the VMI program, the CRM can now generate a restock proposal automatically based on their order history and current inventory picture. It also keeps sample products in a dedicated section so proposals stay focused and easy to review before sending.

Orders + Proposals
📊 Market Intelligence
📈

Statewide store trends now include a full month-by-month history

The Market tab's trend view goes deeper — you can now scroll back through monthly purchase history for any WA store, giving a longer-term view of how an account's buying has shifted over time.

Market
🔓

Market data now shows even for stores without a full license history on file

Previously, the Market tab required a complete license history to display reported macro data. That requirement has been relaxed so reps see useful state and county context even when a store's history is incomplete in the system.

Market
🏆

Competition winners are now optional, and event reminders have been added

Competition results in the Market view no longer require a winner to be set, so partial or in-progress contests display cleanly. Event reminders have also been added so upcoming market-related dates surface where they're relevant.

Market
🚫

Competitor pricing data and menu scraping have been retired

The Competitor Pricing section and the menu-scraping data source behind it have been removed from the CRM. The Market tab's WA state data provides a more reliable and complete picture of competitive purchasing without requiring scraped menus.

Market
📦 Orders + Proposals
🔁

Sales reps can now close and reopen stores directly from the CRM

Instead of routing through ops, reps can now mark a store as closed or reopen it themselves. The Insights column layout was also made customizable so reps can surface the data that matters most to their route.

Stores
👤

Editing an order no longer drops the rep attribution

When an order was modified after creation, the original rep's name could be overwritten. That's now fixed — edits preserve who placed the order, keeping commission and reporting accurate.

Orders
🏪 Store Detail + Inventory
📱

The Inventory tab now opens the store's inventory modal on a phone

On mobile, tapping into a store's Inventory tab now opens the full inventory modal just like on desktop, so reps working from their phone can check stock levels during a visit without having to switch devices.

Store Detail
🧹

CRM detail pages, tables, and the dashboard got a thorough mobile cleanup

Several detail pages had tables that ran off-screen or wrapped awkwardly on smaller displays. Those layouts have been tightened up so the CRM is easier to navigate on the go, especially during route days.

Mobile
🤖 MWAH Bot + Data Feeds
📡

MWAH Bot can now look up a store's LCB license and CCRS purchase data directly

When a rep or ops team member asks the bot about a retailer's regulatory standing or wholesale purchase history, it can now pull that directly from the CRM's LCB and CCRS data — no manual lookups required.

MWAH Bot
⚡

POSaBIT Accept cards stay active and the GrowFlow daily review posts only once

A bug causing POSaBIT payment cards to become unclickable after a session timeout has been fixed — the card now revalidates automatically. Separately, the daily GrowFlow reconciliation alert was doubling up; it now posts exactly once each morning.

Operations
🔔

Slack store approval messages are reliable again after a silent regression

Store approval notifications in Slack had stopped working correctly after a recent update. The approval cards and their action buttons are fully restored, and failed card actions now recover gracefully instead of dropping silently.

Alerts

New Pennifer lifestyle imagery is live on the Kiss Labs product pages

Fresh editorial photos have been added to the Kiss Labs section of the site — a lifestyle card and hero image in multiple resolutions for clean display across all screen sizes.

⚙️ Behind the scenes
  • Three CRM versions shipped this week — v102.61 (Monday), v102.62 (Tuesday + Thursday re-verify), v102.63 (Thursday) — as Market Intelligence rolled out in stages.
  • Trends, Insights, and Inventory tabs are noticeably faster after a targeted load-path improvement on the data fetch layer.
  • Market filter controls and period-matching logic were corrected so date comparisons and store matching produce consistent results across all Market views.
  • GrowFlow link warning alerts now route through the CRM alert bot rather than posting raw to Slack, keeping the ops channel readable.
  • CRM running on Node 24.22.1 (patched); website Cloud Functions and site tooling both moved to the same patched Node 24 build.
  • Website operational scripts migrated to Firebase Admin 14 APIs; all scripts verified and covered with new unit tests.
  • Website CI now cancels only superseded checks on a PR, not all checks — reducing unnecessary re-run noise on PRs with multiple pushes.
  • Dep wave across both repos: vitest, Playwright, googleapis, google-auth-library, svix, react-dom, hono, ip-address (CRM); csv-parse, sharp, nanoid, qs, protobufjs, morgan, undici (website).
