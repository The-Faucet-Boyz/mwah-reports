Latte Lover is live on gimmemwah.com — full product page, gallery, and all

The Latte Lover launch landed on the public site this week. Shoppers now see a dedicated product page complete with photography, a flavor carousel, and updated product listing. Pearanormal Activity has been retired and all imagery swapped out. Every product page also got its images converted to WebP, cutting load times noticeably across the whole lineup.

Public Website
📋

Activity Log rebuilt — faster, shared with the portal, and ready for what’s next

The CRM’s Activity Log was migrated onto a shared API and rebuilt with React under the hood. It now feeds the same data the buyer portal uses, so both sides stay in sync. Launch monitoring was wired in at the same time, connecting new-flavor tracking directly to account delivery reviews so the team can see adoption in real time.
