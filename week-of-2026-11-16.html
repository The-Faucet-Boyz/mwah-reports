⭐ Highlights
🤖

Auto-proposals got a full overhaul — smarter rules, rep-specific sending, and one clean settings home

The proposal automation system was rebuilt top to bottom this week. Reps can now be set as individual senders, Hooser's copy bank is in, and the sales lead's scenario rules are live. All proposal settings moved into a single redesigned Admin tab so there's no more hunting across screens. And open proposals now close automatically whenever a matching order comes in — less manual cleanup for everyone.

Auto-Proposals
📅

Calendar and tasks are reliable again — interrupted saves recover, stuck meetings clear, nothing gets lost

A focused reliability pass on Calendar and Tasks landed mid-week and finished strong on Saturday. Interrupted Google Calendar saves now recover automatically, task edits are preserved on reconnect, abandoned Google Meet setups get cleared, and the legacy activity write paths were unified so Calendar entries stop going missing. The false "Some items may be missing" warning is gone too.

Calendar + Tasks
🗓️ Calendar + Route planning
🗺️

Scheduling an event now saves your current route

When a rep schedules a store visit, the app now saves the route they were working on at the time. The map legend is also visible on phones, and store-name filters now match on a single word so it's faster to narrow down a list.

Route + Calendar
📋

Task creation is now shared — multi-rep events work correctly

Task creation was unified so multi-rep event setups no longer produce duplicate or missing tasks. Ordinary task edits and linked meeting recovery are also preserved when a save gets interrupted mid-flow.

📦 Proposals + Inventory
📐

Proposal view is tighter — cells condensed and Days Supply simplified

The Proposal layout got a cleanup pass: cells are more compact, and the Days Supply column shows the essential number without extra noise. The view fits more on screen without scrolling.

Proposals
📊

VMI Inventory view has real data throughout — including sent emails and negative sellable

The inventory screen now shows the actual email that was sent when you open "View email," surfaces negative sellable stock correctly, adds a low days-of-supply indicator, and correctly sorts the To Do list. Proposal activities are also recorded in the right place now.

Inventory
🧪 Samples + Events
📋

Multi-store events now track samples per store — not just per event

When a rep runs a demo or tasting across multiple stores, sample quantities are now saved separately for each store rather than as one lumped event total. This gives a cleaner record and correct per-store counts in reporting.

Samples
✅

Samples save reliably when an event completes

Samples are now written atomically when an eligible event is logged, so nothing slips through if the app is closed or connectivity drops mid-save. The workflow that completes a scheduled event also saves samples in the same step.

Samples
👥 Contacts + Portal
🔗

Merging contacts now carries over buyer portal links

When two contacts are merged, the destination contact now inherits all buyer portal access links from the source — previously those would be left behind. Notes and email history also follow in the same merge.

Contacts
💬

Team members see buyer portal chat context in Intercom

When a buyer reaches out via the portal chat, the CRM-side team view in Intercom now shows relevant context from their portal session — making it easier to pick up a conversation with full background.

Buyer Portal
📱 Dashboard + Analytics
📊

Dashboard cards, Top Drivers, and Anomaly Watch all fixed up

Card display states that were showing incorrect loading or empty states are corrected. The "Top Drivers" comparison now correctly reflects last-order-month data, and the Anomaly Watch panel is wired in to flag unusual order-rhythm changes as they appear.

Dashboard
🏪 Store visibility + Alerts
🚫

Closed stores no longer show up on the public locator

Stores that are marked closed in the CRM are now excluded from the public gimmemwah.com store finder. Previously a closed store could still appear on the map for shoppers.

Store Locator
📆

Draft orders no longer affect "Next Delivery Date" on the locator

The "next delivery" date shown on a store's map card now excludes orders that are still in Draft status. Only confirmed and later orders push the date forward — so the info shoppers see is accurate.

Store Locator
🔔

Slack infra alerts got cleaner — identifier-only code blocks and change-triggered posting

The CRM's internal Slack alerts now use code-formatted identifiers instead of mixed prose, include links where relevant, and post on change rather than on every check. Easier to scan at a glance.

Alerts
🌐 Public website
🏷️

Store locator shows the right status pill depending on which flavor view you're in

In the "All MWAH" view, stores that carry both MWAH and Latte Lover now show both status pills side by side. When you switch to a specific flavor view, only that flavor's pill appears — no more mixed signals across views.
